# Regional terms: review before publication

These are regional drafting templates, not certification of UK, EU or US compliance. EU member-state and US state differences require qualified legal review. Country clauses do not impose all three legal systems on every user.

The ZIP contains one changed source file and three matching readable drafts. Replace TermsPage.tsx at the included repository path and deploy the frontend after review. This changes the terms page only; it does not implement legal compliance or bind users automatically.

Before publication, verify:
- Contract acceptance, version records and notice to existing users. Merely displaying terms is not proof of enforceable assent.
- Cancellation/withdrawal forms, purchase confirmation and separate immediate-supply consent where required. The credit/promotions classification needs legal review.
- Actual claim windows, payouts, delivery/refund workflows and correspondence with trader duties. Existing non-refundability notices must be reconciled.
- EU DSA applicability, trader traceability, notices, complaints, product safety and representation/contact requirements. This draft does not substitute for those processes.
- US state balance/gift-card, privacy, tax, seller-verification and marketplace requirements; games/rewards classification across regions.
- International availability, currencies, taxes and seller-versus-platform duties. New checkouts currently charge GBP.
- Company details provided by the owner and the selected governing-law clauses.
